About Us

About Lolwethu's Fast Food Kitchen

Lolwethu's Fast Food Kitchen is a small fast food business based in Gqeberha. The business provides affordable meals for customers who enjoy fast food.

We sell different types of meals including kotas, gatsbys, wings and chips, Russian and chips, and burgers.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide tasty food at reasonable prices and to make sure that our customers enjoy their meals.

Why Choose Us?

  • Different meals to choose from
  • Convenient location
  • Friendly service
  • Freshly prepared food
